ABOUT AMPLIFY

Our story

Amplify is a music store for people who play, record and make things loud.

What we do

We bring together the gear musicians ask for most: electric guitars and amps, studio monitors and microphones, digital pianos and the pedals that shape your tone. Our range includes brands such as Fender, Gibson, Yamaha, Roland, Shure and Ibanez.

How we choose

We keep the range focused. If we would not recommend a piece of gear to a friend, it does not make the shelf. That is why you will find fewer products here than in a giant catalogue, and a bit more confidence about each one.

What you can expect

Honest advice

Straight answers from people who play and record, whether you are buying your first guitar or your fifth monitor.

Clear prices

The price you see is the price you pay, and shipping is shown before you order.

Real stock

Availability is shown on every product, so you know what can ship now.
