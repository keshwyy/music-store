Warranty

Every new product is covered, and you can return it if it is not right for you.

What the warranty covers

All new products come with the 2-year legal guarantee that applies to consumer goods in the European Union. It covers defects that were present when the product was delivered. Many manufacturers add their own warranty on top; its terms are supplied with the product.

What is not covered

  • Normal wear, such as strings, fret wear, pads, cables and batteries
  • Damage caused by accidents, drops, liquids or misuse
  • Repairs or modifications made by anyone other than an authorised service centre

How to make a claim

  1. Contact us with your order number, a short description of the fault and, if possible, a photo or video.
  2. We confirm the next step and arrange the return or repair.
  3. We repair or replace the product, or refund you if neither is possible.

Returns & refunds

You can return an item within 14 days of receiving it, without giving a reason.

  • Tell us you want to return the item through the contact page or by email.
  • Send it back unused and in its original packaging, with all accessories.
  • We refund the price of the item to your original payment method within 14 days of receiving it back.
  • Return shipping is paid by you, unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong one.
